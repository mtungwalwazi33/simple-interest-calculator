# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
The Simple Interest Calculator calculates the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specified period at a fixed annual interest rate.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- Principal is the initial amount of money.
- Rate is the annual interest rate expressed as a percentage.
- Time is the period in years.

## Example
Principal = $1,000
Rate = 5%
Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100
Simple Interest = $100

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest
Total Amount = $1,100

## Features
- Calculates simple interest.
- Shows the total amount after interest.
- Uses a simple mathematical formula.
